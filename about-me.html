About Me

I'm a biomedical and public health data scientist with 3+ years of experience applying machine learning, AI, and statistical modeling across epidemiology, precision medicine, and behavioral health.

My work sits at the intersection of three worlds that rarely meet in one person: the biology (genomics, NGS, variant calling), the clinical reality (OCD, autism, eating disorders, epilepsy), and the modeling that connects them. At Rogers Behavioral Health, I've quantified the healthcare burden of rare genetic diseases, analyzed clinical outcomes and medication use across patient cohorts, and built data pipelines to make messy patient data usable. Earlier, I built ML models for epilepsy research and COVID-19 epidemiology, and reviewed the landscape of AI methods for precision medicine. Across all of it, I care about work that actually improves how patients are diagnosed and treated.
