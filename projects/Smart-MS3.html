A Pilot Study on Postpartum Care

Smart MS3 · Rutgers MBS Externship Program · Survey design & health awareness

An applied public-health project with Smart MS3, a health-technology startup, assessing the challenges women face during the postpartum period and how technology might support their recovery and care.

This project was completed through the Rutgers Master of Business and Science (MBS) Externship Program, which pairs students with companies to tackle real business and research problems. Smart MS3 builds biofeedback tools for the physical-therapy space, where low patient engagement drives high drop-off. Working with a team of three, I helped extend that focus to postpartum recovery — a period with significant unmet needs — by designing a way to learn directly from new mothers and by raising awareness of what a healthy postpartum experience should look like.

What we did

  • Literature review
    Synthesized peer-reviewed articles, clinical resources, and material on women's health to ground the study.
  • Pilot survey design
    Built an anonymous Google Forms survey to capture postpartum experiences, structured so responses could later be compared against the literature to define a project scope.
  • Public-health awareness campaign
    Created Instagram post series to educate new mothers and share resources on healthy postpartum care.

What it involved

The project centered on the applied, stakeholder-facing side of health work: turning an open-ended business question into a concrete research plan, designing data collection for a sensitive health population, considering IRB requirements, and communicating health information to the public. It complements my more technical work by covering the community-facing and study-design side of public health.
